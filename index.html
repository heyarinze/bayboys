What this is

A space to think & chill

Once a month, a few boys sit down for brunch in Oakland and a few other cities across the US to discuss books and essays.

You don't need to read a whole book for the brunch though. Each month, we share a 'Book of the Month' to read at your own pace, and a shorter 'Brunch Brief' you can get through in an hour or two. It might be an essay, a chapter, a podcast episode or a film. The brief is what we'll talk about at the brunch.

To keep things interesting, brunch is hosted at a new spot each month.

Book of the Month · November · Philosophy

Consciencism

Philosophy and Ideology for De-Colonization and Development with particular reference to the African Revolution

Kwame Nkrumah · 1964 · Non-fiction

Written while he was Ghana's first president, Nkrumah asks what a newly free Africa should build its thinking on: its traditional values, Islam, and the Euro-Christian legacy of colonial rule, and how to weave the three into a philosophy of its own.
