What this is

A table for thinking out loud

Once a month, six to ten of us sit down for brunch in Oakland and talk about something that matters: being a man, health, money, art, beauty, and how to live.

Nobody has to finish a book before brunch. Each month has a Book of the Month to read at your own pace, and a short conversation guide you can get through in an hour or two. It might be an essay, a chapter, a podcast episode or a film. The guide is what we talk about at the table.

The table moves to a new spot in Oakland every month, to keep things interesting.

How a brunch runs

Ninety minutes

  1. 11:00

    Plates and coffee. Order, eat, settle in.

  2. 11:15

    Check-in. A minute each on what's on your mind this week.

  3. 11:30

    The guide. This month's Chef frames the topic and opens the conversation.

  4. 12:15

    Takeaways. Everyone names one thing they're leaving with.

  5. 12:25

    The draw. A name comes out of the hat. That's next month's Chef, who picks the topic and writes the guide.
